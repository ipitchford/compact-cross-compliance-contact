# Response to the v2.0 review — 5 October 2026

The supplied review recommends minor revisions. Its identity and external independence are not authenticated. No official REF rating is asserted.

1. Contact-specific comparison: introduction now distinguishes small-friction continuum results, exact half-space cancellation, the Ballard–Iurlano two-dimensional theorem, and the compact-but-nonzero matched bounded-body result. Jarušek's original Theorems 2.2 and 3.1 and Ballard–Iurlano Theorems 2.12–2.13 were inspected. Absence of an equivalent predecessor in this bounded search is not proof of priority.
2. Endpoint measures: an unnumbered lemma before Theorem 4.1 supplies domination, density, capacity representatives and Radon–Nikodym weighted factorisation. It explicitly avoids treating 1/f as a Sobolev multiplier. Original theorem numbers remain stable.
3. Stability: an explicit paragraph fixes A, T and f and excludes material perturbations destroying matching. Operator-property failure remains separate from contact nonexistence.
4. Hierarchy: the introduction explains the common elastic-feedback theme while keeping the prescribed-pressure rate-and-state results separate from unknown-pressure contact.
5. Bibliography: Pipping et al. 2016 DOI corrected to 10.1093/gji/ggv512 in current manuscript, response and source audit. Immutable original input ZIP is retained privately.
6. Corollary 2.3 now states smooth compactly supported radial profiles, sufficient for the later localisation. It no longer quantifies over arbitrary finite-mass profiles.
7. Numerical curvature language now says consistent with restricted local minimality or its failure. No interval certification, global minimality or dynamical stability is claimed.
8. Boundary calculus: added Dyatlov's public notes, Section 3 properties and parametrix construction, alongside Tan–Liu's elastic symbol theorem. Remote separated components, coercive inversion and projections remain explicit.
9. Computational robustness: supporting-code assertions are mechanically replaced by explicit exceptions, retaining their expressions. Optimised Python must execute the same conditions. Mutation tests exercise the sign, matching and low-frequency constants.

Optional further theorems, a practical fully discrete convergence result and interval-certified curvatures are not prerequisites and are not claimed. The unpublished general mismatched and continuous-time problems remain open within this package's scope.
