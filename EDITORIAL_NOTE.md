# Editorial note — v2.0

The title, abstract and contribution statement now lead with actual matched-interface contact existence and the sharp cross-operator compactness classification. The rate-and-state results remain a separately identified prescribed-pressure part.

The referee's optional significance-enhancing request has been pursued analytically, not converted into a promise. Theorem 4.1 proves contact existence from compact cross compliance; Lemma 5.1 and Theorem 5.2 verify that hypothesis for smooth bounded bodies with matching boundary lambda+mu. Corollaries 4.2–4.3 provide solution stability and reaction-space approximation. Theorem 5.3 localises the obstruction to actual bounded-body cross operators and gives an exact classification of the unrestricted dominated pairing property.

The classical elastic-similarity cancellation, Ballard–Iurlano normal-reaction formulation, Brézis existence theorem, and Tan–Liu boundary symbol are credited. The new contribution is not the invention of any of those ingredients. No exhaustive priority claim is made for their present combination.

The reviewer rated v1.0 provisionally at the lower end of 3-star with 2-star downside. That assessment does not automatically carry over, upward or downward, to newly added unreviewed theorems. The revision now addresses the specific missing significance connection to actual contact equations. It is a stronger candidate for reassessment; an achieved 4-star rating is not asserted.

A targeted specialist review should concentrate on the trace-surjectivity/type-(S)+ argument, the compactness conclusion from cancellation of the two smooth boundary symbols, and the localised defect proof. The periodic finite-layer calculation provides an independently checkable concrete realisation of compact but nonzero cross coupling.
