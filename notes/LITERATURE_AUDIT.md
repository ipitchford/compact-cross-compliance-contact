# Literature and attribution audit — 5 October 2026

## Boundary of the search
Targeted primary-source checking, not a systematic or exhaustive priority review. Queries covered logarithmic-Euler rate-and-state steps; the exact Ballard–Iurlano three-dimensional dominated pairing; elastic normal–tangential cross compliance; matched/elastically similar two-body contact; arbitrary-friction existence; smooth elastic boundary symbols. Search tools were web search and Exa, followed by arXiv, publisher and institutional records. Generic contact simulations, electrical Coulomb problems, nonlocal pressure-smoothed laws and frictionless Signorini results were not treated as equivalent theorems. Some general web queries returned irrelevant results; they were not used as evidence.

## Directly inspected load-bearing sources

**Ballard & Iurlano (2024; revised arXiv v2 2026).** Full revised HTML inspected, including normal-reaction formulation, the two-dimensional cross-product theorem, Remark 2.19 and Appendix A. The paper supplies the normal-reaction formulation and established existence machinery. It does not assert the 3D dominated pairing holds. The present candidate credits that framework, supplies compact-cross-compliance hypotheses and verifies them for the specified matched 3D bodies.
https://arxiv.org/html/2303.16298v2
https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218202524500477

**Tan & Liu (2023).** Full arXiv HTML inspected. Theorem 1.1 gives the order-one elastic Dirichlet-to-Neumann principal symbol for smooth variable Lamé coefficients on a smooth boundary. The revised proof explicitly restricts to a separated contact component, inverts the elliptic map using coercivity, and projects normal/tangent parts. Neither the boundary calculus nor the original principal symbol is claimed new.
https://arxiv.org/html/2211.06650v1
https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6420/ace649

**Gräser, Kornhuber & Podlesny (2024).** Publisher PDF inspected, especially equations (20), (21), (43), Propositions 1–2, Problem 3, Section 3.3 and references [30], [33]. The page containing the coupled-existence attribution and the reference page were visually checked. The normal stress is replaced by a prescribed parameter in Section 2.2. The exact shift alpha=alpha_G+log(V_star/L) reproduces the manuscript's two backward-Euler state equations. The state integrator is prior art. Their geometric multibody formulation is broader than the revised fixed-trace rate-and-state theorem in some respects; its assumptions are not silently imported.
https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10596-023-10231-4.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10596-023-10231-4

**Pipping, Sander & Kornhuber (2015).** Public preprint inspected, especially Propositions 4.3–4.4 and Theorem 5.14. The latter is a coupled ageing-law result with b/a strictly below two and old alpha and exp(-alpha) in L2. It does not supply the slip-law statement attributed to it in the later citation chain. This observation narrows an attribution; it is not a refutation of the later numerical paper.
https://www.igpm.rwth-aachen.de/Download/reports/pdf/IGPM359.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1002/zamm.201300062

**Pipping thesis (2014).** Full-text excerpts from the dissertation and the supplied referee audit checked against its author/institutional records. Section 3.4 and Corollary 3.8 establish existence for both laws with an exact frozen-rate state flow, including pure elasticity and arbitrary step size. The direct institutional PDF endpoint was intermittently blocked; the author record and Exa-accessible dissertation text were used. This result is prior art, not a new resolution. The integrator mismatch is made explicit rather than ignored.
https://www.mi.fu-berlin.de/en/math/groups/ag-numerik/theses_neu/pipping/index.html
https://pipping.org/elias/
https://refubium.fu-berlin.de/bitstream/handle/fub188/3568/Pipping_Elias.diss.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y

**Pipping et al. (2016).** Publisher full text retrieved through Exa, especially Section 2.3 and equations (15)–(18). It already explains nonconvexity after eliminating the state and chooses a fixed-point solver. Its midpoint vector-rate exact-flow problem is not automatically the same radial-current-rate reduced functional used here.
https://academic.oup.com/gji/article/204/3/1858/676850
https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggv512

**Brézis (1968).** Primary archive bibliography checked. The precise corollary needed is inspected in the explicit modern restatement in Ballard–Iurlano Appendix A, Theorem A.3, attributed there to Corollary 30. The original 61-page proof was not independently re-audited in this revision.
https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.280/
https://doi.org/10.5802/aif.280

## Related sources with limited inspection

**Argatov & Mishuris (2018), “Tangential contact of elastically similar bodies”.** Institutional abstract/publication record only, not the full chapter. It describes classical half-space decoupling under a relation between elastic constants. Used to credit that physical cancellation, not as evidence that no bounded-body result exists. The matching formula itself is derived in the current manuscript from the full inspected elastic symbol.
https://research.aber.ac.uk/en/publications/tangential-contact-of-elastically-similar-bodies/
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-78533-2_6

**Jarušek (1983), “Contact problems with bounded friction. Coercive case”.** Substantial primary full-text excerpts via Exa, including the general-body and two-body discussion, were screened. They state small-friction conditions; no equivalent compact-cross-compliance arbitrary-friction result was identified in that screening. The direct DML endpoint timed out. The complete paper has not been exhaustively checked for an equivalent special-case corollary.
https://doi.org/10.21136/cmj.1983.101875

**Ballard–Iurlano subsequent 2D evolution work (arXiv:2508.04863).** Screened as related continuous/jumping evolution, not treated as a three-dimensional matched incremental theorem.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04863

**Mielke & Roubíček (2024).** The supplied review screened its abstract/record for a related bulk elastoplastic rate-and-state model. No claim that this revision re-audited its full proof, and no novelty inference is based on it.
https://doi.org/10.4171/IFB/506

## Bounded conclusion
There is definite prior overlap for logarithmic-Euler formulation, arbitrary-step frozen-flow existence, state-elimination nonconvexity, half-space similarity cancellation, the elastic boundary symbol, and the normal-reaction variational framework. Those are credited. The inspected corpus did not supply the exact compact-but-nonzero smooth matched-interface contact theorem and its classification/stability package. That is a candidate novelty claim, not proof of priority. Older monographs, unindexed theses and recent preprints remain material search gaps.
