4\. Well-posedness of rate-and-state friction (fault mechanics)

***Open:***

- Existence and uniqueness are proved only for a viscoelastic (Kelvin–Voigt) body with the ageing law (Pipping, 2019).

- Existence at each time step under Dieterich’s law requires ‖b/a‖<sub>∞</sub> \< 2.

- For sliding between two dissimilar elastic materials, only the linearised, regularised problem is known to be well-posed (Ranjith & Rice, 2001).

- The time-incremental elastic contact problem with Coulomb friction is solved in two dimensions but not three (Ballard & Iurlano, 2024).

***To do:***

- Prove existence for a purely elastic body.

- Cover the slip law and remove the b/a \< 2 restriction.

- Prove nonlinear well-posedness for regularised bimaterial sliding.

- Settle the three-dimensional incremental contact problem.

Each of these is a self-contained problem in partial differential equations (PDEs).
